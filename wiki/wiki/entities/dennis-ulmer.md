# Dennis Ulmer

Researcher working on [[concepts/uncertainty-quantification|uncertainty quantification]] for machine learning, appearing in this wiki as a co-author on the non-exchangeable conformal risk control line of work.

## Affiliation

IT University of Copenhagen.

## Contribution in This Wiki

Co-author of [[sources/farinhas-2024-non-exchangeable-crc|Non-Exchangeable Conformal Risk Control]] (ICLR 2024), with [[entities/antonio-farinhas|António Farinhas]], [[entities/chrysoula-zerva|Chrysoula Zerva]] and [[entities/andre-martins|André F.T. Martins]].

The paper's main result bounds expected loss for bounded, monotonically nonincreasing loss functions under distribution drift, with the bound depending on total variation distance between the observed and test distributions and on a choice of weights. Maximum entropy motivates exponentially decaying weights for time series. See [[concepts/conformal-risk-control|Conformal Risk Control]] and [[concepts/distribution-drift|Distribution Drift]].
