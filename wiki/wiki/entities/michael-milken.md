# Michael Milken

## Notes

Person named in 10 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-1994-how-does-an-inefficient-market-get-that-way|How Does an Inefficient Market Get That Way?]] (1994): He uses a Pensions & Investments article about SEI's recommendation of high yield allocations to show that plan sponsors still shunned junk bonds because of the 1989-90 collapse and the Milken/Drexel association.
- *Everyone Knows* (2007, page not yet in the wiki): He contrasts his early experience with the Nifty Fifty growth stocks at First National City Bank, which collapsed from p/e ratios of 80-90 to 8-9, with Michael Milken's 1978 case for B-rated bonds, which inspired Oaktree's motto about avoiding losers.
