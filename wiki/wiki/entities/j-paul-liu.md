# J. Paul Liu

## Notes

Author of *How to Build and Fine-Tune a Small Language Model* (2025). The book gives no formal title or affiliation. The front matter calls him Dr. The acknowledgements thank NC State's Data Science and AI Academy and Bioinformatics Research Center, and the Research Triangle AI Society, for teaching opportunities. The preface says the book grew from his "Generative AI for Science" course.
