- [[sources/marks-2002-the-realists-creed|The Realist's Creed]] (2002): Spring 2002, after the technology/telecom bubble burst: the NASDAQ had fallen since 2000, capital was denied to start-ups in 2000-02, Enron had collapsed, and investors disappointed by equities were migrating into hedge funds marketed as 'absolute return' vehicles.
