## Notes

Person named in 9 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-1994-risk-in-todays-markets-revisited|"Risk in Today's Markets" Revisited]] (1994): The blow-ups at Soros's Quantum Fund, Steinhardt and Askin's Granite Funds illustrate that labels like 'hedge' or 'market neutral' mean little, that leverage on volatile positions is dangerous, that forced selling degrades portfolio quality, and that incentive fees create asymmetric payoffs for managers.
- [[sources/marks-2000-irrational-exuberance|Irrational Exuberance]] (2000): Marks shows how the late 1990s turned reputations upside down: Greenspan, Julian Robertson, Soros and Druckenmiller, Gary Brinson, Buffett and Jeremy Siegel were dismissed, while the Dow 36,000 authors and a 27-year-old fund manager were lionised.
