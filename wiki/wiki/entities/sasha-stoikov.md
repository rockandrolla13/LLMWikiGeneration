# Sasha Stoikov

Market microstructure researcher whose name is attached to two objects in this wiki. The [[concepts/avellaneda-stoikov-model|Avellaneda–Stoikov model]] of [[concepts/market-making|market making]] sets bid and ask quotes around a reservation price that depends on inventory and risk aversion. And with [[entities/rama-cont|Rama Cont]] and [[entities/arseniy-kukanov|Arseniy Kukanov]] he wrote [[sources/cont-2014-price-impact-order-book-events|The Price Impact of Order Book Events]], which defined [[concepts/order-flow-imbalance|order flow imbalance]] and showed that mid-price changes are linear in it with a slope inverse to book depth.

The 2014 paper also cites earlier work by Avellaneda, Stoikov and Reed on forecasting prices from Level 1 quotes in the presence of hidden liquidity, which is the concern behind its caveat that hidden orders are one reason the stylised depth model only holds statistically.

Affiliation is not stated in the arXiv version of the paper held here.
