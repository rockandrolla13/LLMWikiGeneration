# MiniMind

## Notes

Open-source project for training small language models from scratch, by [[entities/jingyao-gong|Jingyao Gong]] (github.com/jingyaogong/minimind). The original targets Chinese with a 6,400-token vocabulary. Liu (2025) adapts it to English with a 32,000-token BPE and uses a decoder-only design with RoPE, grouped-query attention, RMSNorm and SwiGLU. The book lists 26M, 109M and 350M variants.
