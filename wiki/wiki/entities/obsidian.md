# Obsidian

## Notes

A markdown note-taking and knowledge-base application. Notes are plain-text markdown files stored locally, with a plugin ecosystem, optional paid Sync with end-to-end encryption and Publish. Its CEO is @kepano, author of the "File over app" essay. This wiki is an Obsidian vault.

Andrej Karpathy switched his personal notes to Obsidian in February 2024 and later used it as the front end for his LLM knowledge bases, viewing raw data, the compiled wiki and outputs such as Marp slides in it. By March 2025 he used it for concrete projects rather than day-to-day notes.
