# Andrew Marks

## Notes

Person named in 23 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-2002-etorres-wisdom|Etorre's Wisdom]] (2002): Prompted by his son asking why he drives in the slow lane, Marks uses 'Etorre's Observation' (the other line moves faster) as a metaphor for efficient markets.
- [[sources/marks-2009-will-it-work|Will It Work?]] (2009): Prompted by his son Andrew asking whether Treasury Secretary Geithner was doing the right things, Marks argues that economics is not an exact science and that there is no 'golden tool' for fixing the 2008-09 crisis.
