# Ben Bernanke

## Notes

Person named in 14 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-2008-whodunit|Whodunit]] (2008): CDO investors had lost over $100 billion, monoline insurers' triple-A ratings were threatened, and the Fed under Bernanke had made a 3/4-point cut on January 21 followed by another 1/2 point a week later.
- [[sources/marks-2008-plan-b|Plan B]] (2008): He supports the Treasury plan, trusts Bernanke and Paulson, discusses the pricing, ownership, compensation and political obstacles, and argues that a short-selling ban is acceptable short-term but not long-term.
