# John Kenneth Galbraith

## Notes

Person named in 32 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-2005-there-they-go-again|There They Go Again]] (2005): Opening with Galbraith on 'the extreme brevity of the financial memory', Marks catalogues the recurring mistakes that drive bubbles, from 'it's different this time' to 'if it stops working, I'll get out'.
- [[sources/marks-2022-bull-market-rhymes|Bull Market Rhymes]] (2022): Recurring features included a 'new, new thing' that history cannot discount (FAAMG super stocks, unprofitable IPOs, SPACs, Robinhood and meme stocks, crypto), Galbraith's brevity of financial memory, a race to the bottom in lending standards, and rising leverage.
