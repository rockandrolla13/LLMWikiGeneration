# Barclays

## Notes

Bank named in 4 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-2011-on-regulation|On Regulation]] (2011): He finds post-crisis US regulation surprisingly mild, and shows how the Volcker Rule, executive hedging of stock grants and Barclays' reclassification of its US unit illustrate a 'balloon' effect where self-interest pops out elsewhere.
- *The Calculus of Value* (2025, page not yet in the wiki): Applying this, he finds fundamentals weaker than at the start of 2025 after the April tariffs, yet prices higher, with record price-to-sales, record market cap to GDP, a Barclays euphoria gauge at bubble levels and near-record tight credit spreads.
