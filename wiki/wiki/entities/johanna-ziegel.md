# Johanna Ziegel

**Johanna Ziegel** is a researcher affiliated with ETH Zurich. Co-developer of the Fissler-Ziegel joint elicitability framework for VaR and Expected Shortfall.

## Affiliation

- ETH Zurich

## Publications in This Wiki

- [[sources/wang-2022-e-backtesting|E-backtesting]] (Management Science, 2025) — co-author with [[entities/qiuqi-wang|Qiuqi Wang]] and [[entities/ruodu-wang|Ruodu Wang]]. A model-free, anytime-valid backtest for [[concepts/expected-shortfall|Expected Shortfall]]. The paper leans on her joint elicitability work: because ES is not elicitable on its own, the VaR forecast is carried as auxiliary information, using the Fissler–Ziegel result that the pair is jointly elicitable.
- [[sources/barendse-2026-efficient-tail-interquantile|Efficiently Weighted Estimation of Tail and Interquantile Expectations (2026)]] (cited)
