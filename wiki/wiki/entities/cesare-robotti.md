# Cesare Robotti

Researcher in empirical asset pricing, appearing in this wiki as a co-author on work testing whether corporate bond returns support multifactor pricing.

## Affiliation

Warwick Business School.

## Contribution in This Wiki

Co-author of [[sources/dickerson-2023-bond-risk|Priced Risk in Corporate Bonds]] (Journal of Financial Economics, 2023), with Alexander Dickerson and Philippe Mueller.

The paper argues the bond CAPM is not dominated by more elaborate factor models: previously proposed bond risk factors show no incremental explanatory power over the market factor, and only traded liquidity shows marginal additional power. It is a negative result about factor proliferation in corporate bonds.
