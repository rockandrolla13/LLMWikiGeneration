# Winston Churchill

## Notes

Person named in 5 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- *Economic Reality* (2016, page not yet in the wiki): He ends with lists of what governments can and cannot do and a Churchill quote about lifting oneself by a bucket handle.
- *Growing the Pie* (2019, page not yet in the wiki): Borrowing Ray Dalio's line that populists of the right don't know how to divide the pie while populists of the left don't know how to grow it, he argues that capitalism created the biggest pie, at the price of the unequal sharing Churchill called its 'inherent vice'.
