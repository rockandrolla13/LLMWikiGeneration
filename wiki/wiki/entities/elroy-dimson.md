# Elroy Dimson

## Notes

Person named in 6 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-2009-touchstones|Touchstones]] (2009): Using Dimson's 'risk means more things can happen than will happen', he argues for treating the future as a range of outcomes and building in a margin of safety.
- [[sources/marks-2014-risk-revisited|Risk Revisited]] (2014): He makes four key points: the future is a range of possibilities, 'risk means more things can happen than will happen' (Dimson), knowing probabilities is not knowing outcomes, and although many things can happen, only one will, so intolerable outcomes must be avoided even on high expected-value paths.
