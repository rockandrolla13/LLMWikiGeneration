# Lehman Brothers

**Lehman Brothers Holdings Inc.** was a global financial services firm and one of the largest investment banks in the United States until its bankruptcy in September 2008.

## Quantitative Credit Research

Lehman's Quantitative Credit Research (QCR) group produced influential research on credit derivatives and corporate credit markets, including:

- **[[concepts/lever-score|LEVER Framework]]** (2006): Scoring LBO and leveraged recapitalization risk
- **Base Correlation Mapping** (2007): Pricing bespoke CDO tranches
- **CMetrics Framework**: Financial analysis and valuation methodology

## Quantitative Portfolio Strategy

A separate group from QCR, headed by [[entities/lev-dynkin|Lev Dynkin]]. With Robeco it introduced **[[concepts/duration-times-spread|DTS (Duration Times Spread)]]**, a registered service mark of Lehman Brothers, using the Lehman Brothers Credit Index history. The paper states that work was under way to add DTS to the credit components of the Lehman Brothers multi-factor risk model and performance attribution.

- [[entities/arik-ben-dor|Arik Ben Dor]] · [[entities/lev-dynkin|Lev Dynkin]] · [[entities/jay-hyman|Jay Hyman]]

## Historical Note

Despite producing sophisticated quantitative research, Lehman Brothers filed for bankruptcy on September 15, 2008, in the largest bankruptcy filing in U.S. history. The firm's research and methodologies were subsequently absorbed by Barclays and Nomura.
