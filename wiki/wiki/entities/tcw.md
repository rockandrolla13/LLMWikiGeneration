# TCW

## Notes

Investment firm named in 8 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-1993-the-value-of-predictions|The Value of Predictions, or Where'd All This Rain Come From?]] (1993): Marks, then at TCW, sets out every objection he has to investing on the basis of macro and market forecasts.
- [[sources/marks-1994-risk-in-todays-markets|Risk in Today's Markets]] (1994): Written while Marks was still at TCW, this short client memo argues that two trends -- thirty-year-low interest rates and the strong 1991-93 returns earned by risk-takers -- have pushed investors to accept more risk across the whole system, often without realising it, at a time when valuations are already high.
