# AIG

## Notes

Company named in 6 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- *Nobody Knows* (2008, page not yet in the wiki): September 2008, the peak of the global financial crisis: the failure or rescue of Lehman Brothers, Bear Stearns, Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac and AIG; doubts about Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley; T-bill yields near zero; and rising CDS prices on US Treasuries.
- *Plan B* (2008, page not yet in the wiki): September 2008, at the peak of the global financial crisis: Bear Stearns, IndyMac, Lehman Brothers, AIG, Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac had failed or been rescued, Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley became bank holding companies, money market funds were backstopped, and the TARP proposal was being debated six weeks before the US presidential election.
