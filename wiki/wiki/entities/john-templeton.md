# John Templeton

## Notes

Person named in 10 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- *This Time It's Different* (2019, page not yet in the wiki): Marks reopens Anise Wallace's October 11, 1987 New York Times article on John Templeton's 'four most dangerous words', which appeared eight days before Black Monday.
- *Further Thoughts on Sea Change* (2023, page not yet in the wiki): Templeton allowed that 'this time is different' might be true 20% of the time; Marks argues the post-2022 rate regime is such a case.
