# Lev Dynkin

Co-author of [[sources/bendor-2007-dts|DTS (Duration Times Spread)]]. The paper cites his co-authored book, Dynkin, Gould, Hyman, Konstantinovsky and Phelps, *Quantitative Management of Bond Portfolios* (Princeton University Press, 2006), for the Lehman Brothers risk model and performance attribution methodology.

Affiliation as stated in the paper: Head of Quantitative Portfolio Strategy, [[entities/lehman-brothers|Lehman Brothers]].
