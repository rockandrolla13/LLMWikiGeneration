# Jean-Philippe Bouchaud

Physicist and the central figure of the econophysics account of price formation. The position he argues, across decades of work and restated in [[sources/maitrier-2026-square-root-impact-framework|the unifying framework paper]], is the **order-driven** view: prices move because people trade, and volatility is generated mechanically by trading rather than by revelation of fundamental value.

The technical apparatus supporting that claim is largely his: the [[concepts/propagator-model|propagator model]], the empirical case for the [[concepts/square-root-law|square-root law]] of [[concepts/metaorder|metaorder]] impact, latent liquidity theory, and the reconciliation of long-memory order flow with diffusive prices.

He is unusually explicit about what remains unexplained — the 2025 framework paper declines to derive the square-root law, calling it an incontrovertible empirical fact still awaiting a convincing explanation.

Affiliations as stated in the 2025 papers: Capital Fund Management; Chair of Econophysics and Complex Systems, École polytechnique; Académie des Sciences.
