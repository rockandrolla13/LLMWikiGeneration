# Freddie Mac

## Notes

Government agency named in 9 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-2008-what-worries-me|What Worries Me]] (2008): Late August 2008, weeks before the Lehman bankruptcy: Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac had just been rescued, subprime and CDO losses had damaged U.S. financial exports, oil had hit $148 earlier in the year and then fallen about 20% in six weeks, sovereign wealth funds were recapitalising banks, and a U.S. presidential election campaign was under way.
- [[sources/marks-2008-nobody-knows|Nobody Knows]] (2008): September 2008, the peak of the global financial crisis: the failure or rescue of Lehman Brothers, Bear Stearns, Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac and AIG; doubts about Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley; T-bill yields near zero; and rising CDS prices on US Treasuries.
