# Bear Stearns

## Notes

Bank named in 14 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-2007-its-all-good|It's All Good]] (2007): He predicts rising defaults and a large distressed LBO opportunity, lists what might end the upswing, and notes the first cracks: subprime delinquencies, two failed Bear Stearns funds, and pulled low-grade debt deals.
- [[sources/marks-2007-its-all-good-really|It’s All Good . . . Really?]] (2007): Subprime delinquencies and foreclosures were rising, Bear Stearns told investors its funds were worthless, bridge loans for buyouts such as Chrysler and Boots were hung, the Dow had just had its worst week in years, and CDO formation was seizing up.
