# Sina Ehsani

Asset-pricing researcher. With [[entities/juhani-linnainmaa|Juhani Linnainmaa]] he wrote [[sources/ehsani-2022-factor-momentum|Factor Momentum and the Momentum Factor]]. The paper argues that individual stock momentum is largely the aggregation of autocorrelation in factor returns, which makes momentum a way of [[concepts/factor-timing|timing other factors]] rather than a distinct factor.

The 2022 paper also cites his earlier work with Mehdi Beyhaghi (2017) on the cross-section of expected returns in the secondary corporate loan market, which documents momentum in corporate loans, and a working paper with Linnainmaa on "time-series efficient" factors (2020).

Affiliations as stated in the 2022 paper: Northern Illinois University; consultant to Research Affiliates.
