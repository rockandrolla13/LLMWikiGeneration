# John Bogle

## Notes

Person named in 6 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-2004-hey-steward|Hey, Steward!!]] (2004): A follow-up to his October memo 'The Feeling's Mutual', this piece uses the 2003-04 mutual fund scandals to argue that money managers have drifted from stewardship to salesmanship, borrowing Jack Bogle's phrase.
- [[sources/marks-2018-investing-without-people|Investing Without People]] (2018): He traces indexing from his Chicago professors through Malkiel and Bogle, and argues that passive investing free-rides on the price discovery of active investors, so its wisdom depends on some people still investing actively.
