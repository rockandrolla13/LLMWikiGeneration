# Pensions & Investments

## Notes

Publication named in 4 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-1994-how-does-an-inefficient-market-get-that-way|How Does an Inefficient Market Get That Way?]] (1994): He uses a Pensions & Investments article about SEI's recommendation of high yield allocations to show that plan sponsors still shunned junk bonds because of the 1989-90 collapse and the Milken/Drexel association.
- *The Outlook for Equities* (2013, page not yet in the wiki): Triggered by a Pensions & Investments line that 'the long-term equity risk premium is typically between 4.5% and 5%', Marks attacks investment generalisations and the use of the present tense for the equity risk premium.
