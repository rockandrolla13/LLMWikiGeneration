# Pavel V. Shevchenko

Researcher in quantitative finance, appearing in this wiki as a co-author on work linking commodity futures dynamics to the yield curve.

## Affiliation

Macquarie University.

## Contribution in This Wiki

Co-author of [[sources/he-2024-functional-regression|Multi-Factor Function-on-Function Regression of Bond Yields on WTI Commodity Futures]] (2024, arXiv, submitted to the Journal of Commodity Markets), with Peilun He, [[entities/gareth-peters|Gareth W. Peters]] and Nino Kordzakhia.

The paper extends the [[concepts/schwartz-smith-model|Schwartz-Smith two-factor model]] for commodity futures by bringing yield curve dynamics in through [[concepts/functional-data-analysis|functional regression]]. The motivation is that commodity futures and the yield curve both respond to economic conditions, so modelling commodity markets in isolation discards information.
