# Mihai Cucuringu

Statistician working at the intersection of network analysis, spectral methods and market microstructure. He appears on both halves of the modern [[concepts/order-flow-imbalance|OFI]] literature: [[sources/cont-2023-cross-impact-ofi|the integrated and cross-impact paper]] with Cont and Zhang, and [[sources/sitaru-2023-decomposed-ofi|the event-type decomposition]] with Sitaru and Calinescu.

The network framing is characteristic of his contribution — cross-impact coefficient matrices analysed as weighted directed graphs, with sector structure, degree centrality and singular value spectra used to read what the coefficients mean rather than just how large they are.

Affiliations as stated in the 2023 papers: Department of Statistics and Mathematical Institute, University of Oxford; Oxford-Man Institute of Quantitative Finance; The Alan Turing Institute.
