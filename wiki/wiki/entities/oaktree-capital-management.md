# Oaktree Capital Management

## Notes

Investment firm whose clients receive Howard Marks's memos; the memo archive is hosted on its website. Per *Oaktree at Ten* (April 2005), it opened on April 10, 1995, after Marks and his colleagues left TCW, and it had reached ten years and $28 billion by that memo. That memo gives the founding motto that if the firm avoids the losers, the winners will take care of themselves, and says the firm works only in inefficient markets.
