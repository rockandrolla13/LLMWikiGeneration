# Rama Cont

Probabilist working on market microstructure and the mathematics of limit order books. His work with Kukanov and Stoikov (2014) introduced **order flow imbalance** as the variable that explains short-horizon price changes, replacing trade-based imbalance measures — the construction that the whole [[concepts/order-flow-imbalance|OFI]] literature extends.

With [[entities/mihai-cucuringu|Cucuringu]] and [[entities/chao-zhang|Zhang]] he returned to the problem in [[sources/cont-2023-cross-impact-ofi|Cross-Impact of Order Flow Imbalance in Equity Markets]], introducing integrated OFI and showing that [[concepts/cross-impact|cross-impact]] largely disappears once a stock's own book is read across depth.

He has also worked on Markovian and SPDE models of limit order book dynamics, optimal order placement, and — with Sirignano — deep learning approaches to universal features of price formation.

Affiliations as stated in the 2023 paper: Mathematical Institute, University of Oxford; Oxford Suzhou Centre for Advanced Research.
