# Northern Rock

## Notes

Bank named in 4 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-2008-now-what|Now What?]] (2008): Subprime defaults had led to CDO downgrades from July 2007, margin calls, SIV and conduit failures, bank write-downs and capital raises (Citigroup from Abu Dhabi), Northern Rock's rescue, and a credit crunch by mid-November.
- *Lessons from Silicon Valley Bank* (2023, page not yet in the wiki): Digital herding (social media, online withdrawals) accelerates bank runs: over a third of SVB's deposits left in one day, versus days of queues at Northern Rock in 2007.
