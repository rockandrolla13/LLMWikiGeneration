# Ziyu Xu

Co-author, with [[entities/ruodu-wang|Ruodu Wang]] and [[entities/aaditya-ramdas|Aaditya Ramdas]], of two papers in this wiki that apply e-values where adaptivity breaks classical guarantees:

- [[sources/xu-2021-bandit-multiple-testing|A unified framework for bandit multiple testing]] — FDR control at every stopping time under any sampling rule.
- [[sources/xu-2024-post-selection-evalue-ci|Post-selection inference for e-value based confidence intervals]] — the e-BY procedure, valid under any dependence and any selection rule.

Affiliation as stated in the papers: Carnegie Mellon University, Statistics and Machine Learning.
