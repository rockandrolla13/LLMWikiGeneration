# Juhani T. Linnainmaa

Asset-pricing researcher. With [[entities/sina-ehsani|Sina Ehsani]] he wrote [[sources/ehsani-2022-factor-momentum|Factor Momentum and the Momentum Factor]]. That paper shows that factor momentum spans stock, industry and [[concepts/residual-momentum|residual momentum]], and it reinterprets residual momentum as momentum in factors the investor's model leaves out.

The paper also cites his related work: Arnott, Clements, Kalesnik and Linnainmaa (2021) on short-term cross-sectional factor momentum and industry momentum, and Ehsani and Linnainmaa (2020) on "time-series efficient" factors, which span UMD unconditionally.

[[sources/graef-2025-firm-specific-systematic-momentum|Graef, Hoechle & Schmid (2025)]] target the transmission mechanism of the 2022 paper directly.

Affiliations as stated in the 2022 paper: Dartmouth College; NBER; Kepos Capital. The paper also discloses a shareholding in Research Affiliates and past consulting for Citadel and Research Affiliates.
