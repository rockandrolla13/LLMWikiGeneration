# The Wall Street Journal

## Notes

Publication named in 7 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-1990-the-route-to-performance|The Route to Performance]] (1990): Written in October 1990 as an addendum to a third-quarter client letter, prompted by a late-September Wall Street Journal article about a well-known money manager lagging the S&P 500 and a conversation with a pension plan director.
- [[sources/marks-1996-the-value-of-predictions-ii|The Value of Predictions II (or "Give That Man a Cigar")]] (1996): Marks follows up his 1993 memo on forecasting by checking a handful of semi-annual Wall Street Journal economist surveys he kept in his desk drawer.
