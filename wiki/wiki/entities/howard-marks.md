# Howard Marks

## Notes

Howard Marks is the author of the 165 memos in this collection, written for his clients. The earliest is dated October 12, 1990 and the latest November 11, 2026. The early memos predate Oaktree: the February 1993 and February 1994 memos are signed from TCW. In *Oaktree at Ten* (April 2005) Marks writes that he and his colleagues left TCW to start Oaktree, which opened on April 10, 1995.
