# Richard Bookstaber

## Notes

Person named in 5 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-2000-investment-miscellany|Investment Miscellany]] (2000): The centrepiece summarises Richard Bookstaber's framework of market crises as liquidity events: liquidity demanders value time over price, liquidity suppliers vanish in a crisis, prices become 'countereconomic', and diversification fails because assets held by the same traders become correlated.
- [[sources/marks-2004-the-happy-medium|The Happy Medium]] (2004): He borrows Richard Bookstaber's liquidity framework to explain selling panics and adds the idea of buying panics such as 1999.
