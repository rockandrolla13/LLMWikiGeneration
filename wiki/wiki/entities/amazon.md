# Amazon

## Notes

Company named in 7 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- *Growing the Pie* (2019, page not yet in the wiki): His main case is New York's loss of Amazon's HQ2: he argues the $3 billion in incentives were contingent future tax credits, not money that could be spent elsewhere, so rejecting the deal forfeited jobs and roughly $24 billion of net taxes.
- *Selling Out* (2022, page not yet in the wiki): January 2022, after the strong 2020-21 recovery from the pandemic decline, with highly appreciated growth stocks (e.g., Amazon at $3,304) raising the question of whether to take profits.
