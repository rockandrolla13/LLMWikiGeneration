# Arseniy Kukanov

Co-author, with [[entities/rama-cont|Rama Cont]] and [[entities/sasha-stoikov|Sasha Stoikov]], of [[sources/cont-2014-price-impact-order-book-events|The Price Impact of Order Book Events]], the paper that introduced [[concepts/order-flow-imbalance|order flow imbalance]] as a single variable summarising the effect of limit orders, market orders and cancellations on the mid-price. The paper's linear impact model, and its result that the impact coefficient is inversely proportional to best-quote depth, are the base every later OFI extension in this wiki builds on.

Affiliation is not stated in the arXiv version of the paper held here.
