# Chrysoula Zerva

Researcher working on [[concepts/uncertainty-quantification|uncertainty quantification]] and [[concepts/conformal-prediction|conformal prediction]], appearing in this wiki as a co-author on the non-exchangeable conformal risk control line of work.

## Affiliation

Instituto Superior Técnico, Universidade de Lisboa.

## Contribution in This Wiki

Co-author of [[sources/farinhas-2024-non-exchangeable-crc|Non-Exchangeable Conformal Risk Control]] (ICLR 2024), with [[entities/antonio-farinhas|António Farinhas]], [[entities/dennis-ulmer|Dennis Ulmer]] and [[entities/andre-martins|André F.T. Martins]].

That paper unifies two prior lines — non-exchangeable conformal prediction and conformal risk control — to bound the expected value of a monotone loss when the data distribution drifts over time, rather than assuming [[concepts/exchangeability|exchangeability]]. See [[concepts/conformal-risk-control|Conformal Risk Control]] and [[concepts/non-exchangeable-conformal-prediction|Non-Exchangeable Conformal Prediction]].
