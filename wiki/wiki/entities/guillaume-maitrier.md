# Guillaume Maitrier

Lead author of the two-paper sequence reconciling the [[concepts/square-root-law|square-root law]], order imbalance and volatility — [[sources/maitrier-2026-square-root-impact-framework|the theory]] with Bouchaud, and [[sources/maitrier-2025-artificial-market-generator|the artificial market generator]] with Loeper and Bouchaud.

Also first author of the "double square-root law" study using Tokyo Stock Exchange data with real trader identifiers, which established that instantaneous child-order impact scales as the square root of child volume in addition to the parent-level law — the empirical input the generalized [[concepts/propagator-model|propagator]] is built on.

Affiliations as stated in the 2025 papers: LadHyX UMR CNRS 7646 and Chair of Econophysics and Complex Systems, École polytechnique; BNP Paribas Global Markets.
