# David Swensen

## Notes

Person named in 9 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- *Dare to Be Great II* (2014, page not yet in the wiki): Outperformance requires a portfolio unlike others', which means 'uncomfortably idiosyncratic' positions (Swensen) and accepting the risk of being different and worse.
- *I Beg to Differ* (2022, page not yet in the wiki): He revisits second-level thinking, contrarianism done at a second level (not knee-jerk), and the decision to risk being wrong, using David Swensen's Yale endowment as a case of 'uncomfortably idiosyncratic' success.
