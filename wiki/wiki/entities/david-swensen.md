## Notes

Person named in 9 of the 165 Howard Marks memos in the collection.

Context from the memos:

- [[sources/marks-2014-dare-to-be-great-ii|Dare to Be Great II]] (2014): Outperformance requires a portfolio unlike others', which means 'uncomfortably idiosyncratic' positions (Swensen) and accepting the risk of being different and worse.
- [[sources/marks-2022-i-beg-to-differ|I Beg to Differ]] (2022): He revisits second-level thinking, contrarianism done at a second level (not knee-jerk), and the decision to risk being wrong, using David Swensen's Yale endowment as a case of 'uncomfortably idiosyncratic' success.
