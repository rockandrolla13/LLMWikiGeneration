# Qiuqi Wang

First author of [[sources/wang-2022-e-backtesting|E-backtesting]], with [[entities/ruodu-wang|Ruodu Wang]] and [[entities/johanna-ziegel|Johanna Ziegel]]. The paper builds the first model-free, anytime-valid backtest for [[concepts/expected-shortfall|Expected Shortfall]], the risk measure Basel adopted in place of Value-at-Risk.

Affiliation as stated in the paper: Georgia State University.
