# Empirical Studies on the Cross-Section of Corporate Bond and Stock Markets

**Author:** Jeroen van Zundert · **Year:** 2018 · **Venue:** CentER Dissertation Series, Tilburg University (PhD)

The parent document for [[sources/van-zundert-2017-volatility-momentum|Volatility-Adjusted Momentum]], which is its Chapter 4. Held here as the umbrella source so the individual chapters have somewhere to hang.

## What It Covers

Four substantive chapters, by the author's own descriptions:

- **Chapter 3** — the link between the prices of a firm's stocks and its corporate bonds.
- **Chapter 4** — a refined version of the standard cross-sectional momentum strategy. This is the chapter cited elsewhere in this wiki; see [[sources/van-zundert-2017-volatility-momentum|Volatility-Adjusted Momentum]].
- **Chapter 5** — momentum spilling over from one asset class to another.
- **Chapter 6** — size, value, momentum and low-risk anomalies.

The through-line is the cross-section: whether the effects documented in equities carry into corporate bonds, and whether the standard construction methods are the right ones.

## Why It Is Here

Two reasons to keep the thesis rather than only the chapter.

The chapters cite each other, so a claim in Chapter 4 sometimes rests on a result established in Chapter 3. Reading the chapter alone can leave that dependency invisible.

It also connects to work already in this wiki: van Zundert co-authored [[sources/houweling-2017-factor-investing|Houweling & van Zundert (2017)]] on factor investing in corporate bonds, and Chapter 6's anomaly set is the same territory.

## Caveat

A dissertation is not peer-reviewed in the way its constituent articles later are. Where a chapter was subsequently published, the published version is the one to cite.
