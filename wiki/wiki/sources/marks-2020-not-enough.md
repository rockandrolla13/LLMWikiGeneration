# Not Enough

**Author:** Howard Marks · **Date:** June 11, 2020 · **Type:** memo (article) · **Original:** [oaktreecapital.com](https://www.oaktreecapital.com/docs/default-source/memos/not-enough.pdf)

## Summary

A short, non-investment memo written after the killing of George Floyd and the protests that followed. Marks says that good intentions are not enough and that he rejects the status quo of racial injustice. He lists inequities faced by Black Americans and other people of color in policing, incarceration, education, jobs, health care, housing and Covid-19 outcomes. He describes the individual human costs behind the statistics. He links racial justice to wider economic inequality. He argues that growing the economic pie is not enough and that its division must be repaired, through public education, access to jobs and a more progressive tax system. He also says the free market of theory must be adapted for modern life. He closes by describing Oaktree's diversity and inclusion work, a listening meeting, a charitable giving and matching programme, and his personal pledge to fight systemic racism.

## Key Ideas

1. Holding good thoughts and being inclusive personally is not enough; active steps to dismantle systemic racism are needed.
2. The late-20th-century economic tide that lifted nations did not lift all people, and the gains are now more unevenly shared.
3. Advantages from education and capital are being magnified, and the resulting inequality of outcomes is no longer acceptable.
4. Free markets allocate resources well technically, but some degrees of disparity cannot be accepted, so the market must be adapted.
5. Policy priorities named: better public education, fewer barriers to jobs and a more progressive tax system.
6. Oaktree formalized a diversity and inclusion strategy in 2017 and is adding listening sessions and a charitable matching programme.

## Market Context

June 2020, weeks after George Floyd's killing in Minneapolis, during protests across the US and worldwide and during the Covid-19 pandemic. The memo does not discuss markets.

## Notable Claims

- Oaktree formalized its diversity and inclusion strategy in 2017.
- Growing the pie is insufficient; distribution must be fixed.
- Marks will commit personal resources to combating systemic racism.

## Quotes

> But I now know that’s not enough.

> I feel deeply for every individual forced to live under these conditions. But I know I must do more than simply feel.

> Each of us must commit to playing an active part in dismantling systemic racism and bringing about equality.
