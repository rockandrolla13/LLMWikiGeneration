# Equity Short Interest

## Definition

The fraction of a company's shares outstanding that is on loan, used as a proxy for short sellers' negative views. Ben Dor et al. find that high short interest predicts rating downgrades and lower corporate bond returns once spread, DTS and sector are matched. It works mainly as a one-sided signal that identifies future underperformers.
