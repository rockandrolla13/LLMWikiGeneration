# Low-Risk Anomaly

## Definition

The finding that lower-risk securities earn higher risk-adjusted returns than higher-risk ones. The usual explanation is that leverage-constrained investors bid up risky assets. Ben Dor et al. find it within equities, within credit (short-dated bonds against long-dated bonds) and across a firm's capital structure.
