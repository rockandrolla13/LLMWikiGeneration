# Equity Momentum in Credit

## Definition

A corporate-bond signal that ranks issuers by recent stock-price momentum, then buys the bonds of strong-momentum issuers and sells those of weak-momentum issuers. Ben Dor et al. blend short look-back windows of stock returns and attribute the effect mainly to bond prices reacting more slowly than stocks to news. They also split it into a within-sector issuer signal and a sector-timing signal.
