# Gross Profitability

## Definition

Gross profits divided by total assets, following Novy-Marx (2013). It measures the productivity of the whole asset base, so it speaks to both equity and debt claims. Israel, Palhares and Richardson use it as one of three measures in their credit defensive theme.
