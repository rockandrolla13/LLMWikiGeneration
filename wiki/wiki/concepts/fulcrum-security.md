# Fulcrum Security

## Definition

The most senior security that will take part in a reorganization, where taking part usually means converting into the new equity. Whitman and Diz say control investors accumulate the fulcrum class to steer the plan. Identifying it depends on the enterprise valuation and the proposed plan. In MBIA's 2008 de facto reorganization they identify the common stock as the fulcrum.
