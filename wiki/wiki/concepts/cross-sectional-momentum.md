# Cross-Sectional Momentum

## Definition

A relative strategy ranking instruments by recent performance and going long the strongest and short the weakest, capturing momentum across a universe rather than the time-series direction of one market.

## Is It a Distinct Factor?

[[sources/ehsani-2022-factor-momentum|Ehsani & Linnainmaa (2022)]] argue that much of cross-sectional stock momentum is [[concepts/factor-momentum|factor momentum]] in disguise. Past winners load on factors that did well, so ranking stocks on past returns indirectly times those factors. Adding factor momentum to the five-factor model prices portfolios sorted on t−12 to t−2 returns without rejection, and spans UMD. [[sources/graef-2025-firm-specific-systematic-momentum|Graef, Hoechle & Schmid (2025)]] dispute the mechanism; see [[contradictions/factor-momentum-transmission|Factor momentum transmission]].
