# Limits of Macro Forecasting

## Definition

Marks's long-held view that economic and market forecasts add little value. Forecasts mostly extrapolate the recent past, miss the big turning points that matter, and are rarely right often enough to profit from. Nobody knows the macro future with useful accuracy.
