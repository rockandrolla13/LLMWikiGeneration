# The Pendulum of Investor Psychology

## Definition

Marks's image for how investor attitudes swing between greed and fear, euphoria and depression, risk tolerance and risk aversion. The swing rarely rests at the happy medium in the centre. It spends most of its time moving toward or away from an extreme, and the extremes create the biggest mispricings.
