# LLMOps

## Definition

An operational framework for putting LLM applications into production and keeping them running. It borrows from MLOps and DevOps but adds practices for generative models: data curation, domain adaptation without full fine-tuning, hard evaluation, constant monitoring for drift, and security. Its stated goals are safety, scalability, robustness and reliability.
