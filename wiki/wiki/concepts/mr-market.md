# Mr. Market and the Voting Machine

## Definition

Graham's images for the market's moods: a manic-depressive partner who offers prices every day, and a voting machine in the short run but a weighing machine in the long run. Marks uses them to argue that short-term prices reflect psychology and can be exploited rather than obeyed.
