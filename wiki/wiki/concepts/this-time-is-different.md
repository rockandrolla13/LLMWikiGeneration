# 'This Time Is Different'

## Definition

The belief, common near market peaks, that some new development has suspended the old rules of valuation and cycles. Marks calls 'this time it's different' among the most dangerous words in investing. He links it to Galbraith's observation about the extreme brevity of financial memory.
