# Equity Cushion (Enterprise Value Coverage of Debt)

## Definition

A measure of asset protection that compares a peer-based enterprise value multiple with the issuer's leverage. Enterprise value equals equity market value plus debt minus cash, scaled by adjusted EBITDA. Dividing that multiple by debt/EBITDA shows how many times firm value covers the debt. Kricheff uses it to compare leverage across industries that trade at different multiples.
