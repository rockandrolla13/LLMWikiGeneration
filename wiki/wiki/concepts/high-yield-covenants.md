# High-Yield Covenants

## Definition

The rules in a bond indenture or loan agreement that limit what a leveraged issuer may do. The core negative covenants are debt incurrence (a pro forma leverage or fixed-charge coverage test), restricted payments (a ratio test plus a cumulative basket), change of control (usually a put at 101) and asset sales. Loans add maintenance covenants that the issuer must meet each quarter. Defined terms and carve-outs decide how much protection a covenant really gives.
