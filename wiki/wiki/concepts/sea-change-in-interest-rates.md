# Sea Change in Interest Rates

## Definition

Marks's 2022 argument that the forty-year decline in interest rates, and the easy-money period of 2009-2021, had ended. In a world of higher rates, lenders and credit investors get better returns and borrowers and asset owners lose a tailwind.
