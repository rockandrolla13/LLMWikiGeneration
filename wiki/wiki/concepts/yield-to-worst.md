# Yield-to-Worst and Spread-to-Worst

## Definition

Yield-to-worst is the lowest yield across every call date and the maturity date of a callable bond. Spread-to-worst subtracts the yield of a government benchmark whose maturity matches the worst date. Kricheff treats spread-to-worst as the main measure for comparing high-yield bonds and loans of different maturities. It differs from yield-to-maturity only when the bond trades above a call price.
