# New-Issue Concession

## Definition

The extra spread at which a new corporate bond is offered compared with the issuer's comparable outstanding bonds. Ben Dor et al. find it is explained mostly by deal size and by the level and direction of market spreads, and that it largely disappears soon after issuance. Because new issues enter indices only at month-end, capturing the concession is a source of return that the benchmark does not reflect.
