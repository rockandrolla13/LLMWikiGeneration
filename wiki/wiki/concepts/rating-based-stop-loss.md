# Rating-Based Stop-Loss

## Definition

A mandate rule that forces the sale of any bond downgraded to or below a set rating. Ben Dor et al. find that in diversified corporate portfolios such rules cut returns by more, in percentage terms, than they cut volatility or drawdown. The cost is largest when the threshold sits just below the purchase rating.
