# Global Financial Crisis (2007-2009)

## Definition

The crisis that grew from US subprime mortgage losses into a global banking and credit collapse, including the failure of Lehman Brothers. Marks wrote about it as it unfolded. He traced it to loose lending, structured products, excessive leverage, faulty ratings and complacent psychology.
