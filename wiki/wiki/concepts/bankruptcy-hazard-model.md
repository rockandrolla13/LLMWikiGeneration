# Bankruptcy Hazard Model

## Definition

A discrete-time logistic hazard model of bankruptcy within the next twelve months, following Shumway (2001). It mixes accounting ratios and equity-market variables and is re-estimated on an expanding window, so forecasts stay out of sample. Correia, Richardson and Tuna compare two such models (Beaver-Correia-McNichols and Bharath-Shumway) with distance to default and EDF.
