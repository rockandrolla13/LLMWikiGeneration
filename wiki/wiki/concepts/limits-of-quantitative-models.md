# Limits of Quantitative Models

## Definition

The failure of risk models when their assumptions break. Marks uses Long-Term Capital Management and 2007-08 to show that normal distributions, historical correlations and stable liquidity assumptions fail just when they matter, as correlations go to one in a crisis.
