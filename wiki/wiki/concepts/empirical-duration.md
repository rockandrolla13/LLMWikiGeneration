# Empirical Duration

## Definition

The measured sensitivity of a corporate bond's return to Treasury yield changes, estimated by regressing returns on analytical duration times yield changes. It equals analytical duration scaled by an empirical hedge ratio. Ben Dor et al. find the ratio falls as spreads rise, because rates and spreads tend to move in opposite directions, and that premium and discount bonds behave asymmetrically.
