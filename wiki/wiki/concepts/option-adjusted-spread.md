# Option-Adjusted Spread (OAS)

## Definition

The fixed spread added to the Treasury curve so that a bond's discounted cash flows match its market price, after accounting for embedded options. Israel, Palhares and Richardson use OAS as their carry measure because it isolates the credit component of yield. It equals carry only on a flat credit curve and understates it on an upward-sloping one.
