# Liquidity Cost Score (LCS)

## Definition

A Barclays bond-level estimate of the cost of an immediate round-trip trade of institutional size, as a percentage of price. For spread-quoted bonds it is the bid-offer spread times spread duration. For price-quoted bonds it is the price bid-offer relative to the bid. Ben Dor et al. use it as a transaction-cost estimate in back-tests.
