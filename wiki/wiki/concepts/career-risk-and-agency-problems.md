# Career Risk and Agency Problems

## Definition

Conflicts between investment managers and their clients. Fear of looking wrong pushes managers toward conventional choices, and fees can reward asset gathering over results. Marks discusses fiduciary duty and how institutions can encourage unconventional, long-term decisions.
