# Efficient Market Hypothesis and Inefficient Markets

## Definition

The theory that prices reflect available information, so investors cannot consistently beat the market. Marks accepts it is roughly true for widely followed assets. He argues that neglected, disliked or complex markets such as high yield and distressed debt are inefficient enough for skilled investors to profit, and that inefficiencies shrink as more capital arrives.
