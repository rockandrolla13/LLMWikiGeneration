# Structural Subordination

## Definition

Debt issued at a holding company ranks behind all debt of the operating subsidiaries that own the assets, whatever its stated seniority. Subsidiary guarantees work the other way and can lift new debt ahead of older notes. Kricheff shows how both tools let an issuer prime existing bondholders without breaching a negative pledge.
