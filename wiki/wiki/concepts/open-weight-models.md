# Open-Weight Models

## Definition

Models whose trained weights are publicly released, so they can be deployed or fine-tuned directly. The book separates them from open-source models, which share code and architecture but may not share weights or training data. An open-weight model cannot guarantee what data it was trained on, and licences may restrict commercial use.
