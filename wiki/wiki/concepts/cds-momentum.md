# CDS Momentum

## Definition

Cross-sectional momentum in single-name CDS returns: selling protection on names whose past CDS returns were highest and buying it on those whose returns were lowest. Lee, Naranjo and Sirmans find it is driven by the CDS market anticipating slow-moving rating changes, and that past CDS returns also predict the firm's stock and bond returns.
