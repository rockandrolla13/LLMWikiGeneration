# The Nifty Fifty

## Definition

A group of leading US growth stocks of the late 1960s and early 1970s that investors believed could be bought at any price. Marks worked on them early in his career. They lost most of their value in 1973-74, which he uses as proof that quality does not protect an investor who overpays.
