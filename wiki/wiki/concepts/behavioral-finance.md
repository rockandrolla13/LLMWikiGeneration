# Behavioral Finance

**Behavioral Finance** is the branch of finance studying systematic deviations from rational decision-making by market participants, often using prospect theory, narrow framing, and heuristics.

- [[sources/nolte-2011-fx-latent-factor-panel-intensity|Trading Dynamics in the FX Market: A Latent Factor Panel Intensity Approach (2011)]] — supporting; the FX panel evidence is interpreted through the behavioral-finance lens
