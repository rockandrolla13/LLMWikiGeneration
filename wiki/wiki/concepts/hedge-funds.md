# Hedge Funds

## Definition

Lightly regulated investment funds that charge performance fees and use flexible strategies. Marks's 2004 memo urged caution as capital flooded in, fees stayed high and returns were likely to fall. He doubted that many managers had enough skill to justify the fees.
