# Asset Bubbles

## Definition

Episodes in which prices rise far above any reasonable value because buyers expect to sell to someone else at a higher price. Marks identifies bubbles mainly by investor psychology: universal enthusiasm, new-paradigm stories and the belief that no price is too high. He distinguishes bubbles in genuinely transformational technologies from simple overpricing.
