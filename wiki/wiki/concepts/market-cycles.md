# Market Cycles

## Definition

The recurring rise and fall of economies, profits, asset prices and investor attitudes. Marks argues that cycles are driven largely by human behaviour, so they recur even though their timing and amplitude cannot be predicted. Excesses in one direction plant the seeds of a correction in the other.
