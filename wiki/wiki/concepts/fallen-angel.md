# Fallen Angel

## Definition

A corporate bond downgraded from investment grade to high yield. It leaves investment-grade indices at the next rebalance, so benchmarked and regulated investors must sell it at the same time. Ben Dor et al. show that this forced selling pushes prices below fair value around the downgrade, and that the bonds then tend to recover against high-yield peers over the following months. They separate this price pressure from overreaction, and they rank fallen angels with a Fallen Angel Reversal (FAR) scorecard based on peer-relative spread and the stage of the event.
