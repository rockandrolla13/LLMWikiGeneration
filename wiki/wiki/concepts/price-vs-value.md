# Price vs Intrinsic Value

## Definition

The gap between what an asset costs and what it is worth based on its cash flows and fundamentals. Marks says no asset is so good that it cannot be a bad buy at too high a price, and few are so bad that they cannot be a good buy if cheap enough. Investment success comes from buying things well, not buying good things.
