# The Perversity of Risk

## Definition

The idea that risk is highest when people believe there is none and lowest when everyone thinks something is too risky. Widespread confidence pushes prices up and removes the margin for error, while fear pushes prices down and builds safety in. Risk comes mainly from investor behaviour and price, not asset quality.
