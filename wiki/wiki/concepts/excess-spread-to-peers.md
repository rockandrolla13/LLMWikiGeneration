# Excess Spread to Peers (ESP)

## Definition

A bond-level relative value score. It is the part of a bond's spread over its rating, industry and duration peer group that the issuer's leverage does not explain. Bonds with high ESP are treated as cheap. Ben Dor et al. trade it monthly with entry and exit thresholds, and find returns driven by mean reversion in relative spreads.
