# Two Risks: Losing Money vs Missing Opportunity

## Definition

Marks's point that investors face two risks at once: the risk of losing money and the risk of missing gains. Reducing one increases the other. The pendulum of psychology decides which risk the crowd fears more at any moment, and that choice drives market extremes.
