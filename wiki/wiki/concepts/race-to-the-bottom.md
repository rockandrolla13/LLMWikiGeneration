# The Race to the Bottom

## Definition

Marks's description of how capital providers compete when money is plentiful. Because one lender's money is like another's, they compete by accepting lower returns and weaker protection: looser covenants, higher leverage and less due diligence. The 'winner' of a competitive deal is often whoever accepts the least safety.
