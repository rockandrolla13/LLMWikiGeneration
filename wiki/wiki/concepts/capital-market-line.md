# The Capital Market Line

## Definition

The upward-sloping relationship between risk and expected return across assets, starting at the riskless rate. Marks redraws it with widening ranges of outcomes to show that riskier assets offer higher expected returns but not certain ones. When the line is low and flat, investors get little extra return for extra risk.
