# Taking the Market's Temperature

## Definition

Marks's alternative to forecasting: 'we may never know where we're going, but we'd better know where we are.' He judges whether the market is hot or cold from investor psychology, deal terms and valuations, and calibrates aggressiveness accordingly. This is how he applies 'you can't predict, you can prepare'.
