# Issuer Size Premium

## Definition

The spread or return difference between bonds of small and large issuers, where size is the issuer's debt outstanding in the bond index. Ben Dor et al. find that small issuers' extra spread is mostly explained by rating and liquidity. Once systematic exposures are matched, small issuers underperform large ones, except in crises.
