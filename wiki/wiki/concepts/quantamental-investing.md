# Quantamental Investing

## Definition

The disciplined combination of quantitative signals with fundamental analyst research. Quantitative scores cover the whole universe and point analysts to issuers that score well against sector peers. Analysts then add issuer-level depth that reduces the model's selection risk. Ben Dor et al. build their OneScore composite per sector to match how credit research teams are organised.
