# The Three Stages of a Bull Market

## Definition

Marks's sequence: first a few far-sighted people believe things will get better, then most people realise improvement is under way, and finally everyone concludes things will only get better forever. A mirror sequence describes bear markets. The final stage is the dangerous one.
