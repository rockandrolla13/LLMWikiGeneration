# Factor Timing

**Factor Timing** is the dynamic over/underweighting of style factors (size, value, momentum, profitability, investment) based on conditional forecasts of their risk premia; complements factor investing.

## Sources

- [[sources/cotturo-2026-multifactor-timing-deep-learning|Multifactor Timing with Deep Learning (2026)]] — central; the DMT neural network targets factor returns jointly under a multitask architecture
- [[sources/ehsani-2022-factor-momentum|Factor Momentum and the Momentum Factor (2022)]] — the simplest timing rule, a factor's own past-year sign, predicts its return (51 bps a month after up years against 6 bps after down years); argues stock momentum is itself an indirect factor-timing strategy
