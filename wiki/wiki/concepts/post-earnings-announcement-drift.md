# Post-Earnings-Announcement Drift

## Definition

The tendency of prices to keep moving in the direction of an earnings surprise for weeks or months after the announcement, because investors underreact to the news. Ben Dor et al. show the effect extends to US corporate bonds and is stronger in high yield. Their most predictive surprise measure is the earnings-announcement return: the stock's three-day return around the announcement minus that of its size and book-to-market benchmark portfolio.
