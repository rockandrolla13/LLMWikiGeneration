# Market Timing

**Market Timing** is the trading strategy of switching between risky and risk-free assets based on forecasted return direction or expected return; popular in practitioner literature and tested via Merton-style models.

- [[sources/xie-2026-realized-probability-market-timing|Realized Probability Index is a Better Market Timing Indicator (2026)]] — central; three timing indicators are compared in information content and economic efficiency
