# Convertible Bonds

## Definition

Bonds that can be converted into the issuer's stock, giving bond-like downside and some equity upside. Marks began running convertible portfolios at Citibank in 1978. Supply and demand for the asset class shapes their pricing and their attractiveness, as he discusses in his early memos.
