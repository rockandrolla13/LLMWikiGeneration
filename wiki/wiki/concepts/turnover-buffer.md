# Turnover Buffer

## Definition

A rebalancing rule with separate entry and exit thresholds on a signal. A position is bought only when the signal is strong and sold only when it has clearly weakened, so positions with middling signals are neither bought nor sold. The no-trade zone stops positions flipping around one cut-off and can cut turnover sharply without changing the signal.
