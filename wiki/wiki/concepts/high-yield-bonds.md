# High Yield Bonds

## Definition

Bonds rated below investment grade, which pay higher promised yields to compensate for default risk. Marks began running high yield portfolios at Citibank in 1978. His memos weigh the yield spread against expected credit losses and argue that the asset class is safest to buy when it is most feared.
