# The Credit Cycle

## Definition

The swing of capital markets between a window that is wide open to almost any borrower and one that slams shut. Marks calls it the most volatile cycle because modest changes in the economy get magnified into booms and busts in lending. Easy credit produces high prices and weak deals, and a credit crunch produces bargains.
