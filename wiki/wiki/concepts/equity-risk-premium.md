# Equity Risk Premium and Expected Equity Returns

## Definition

The extra return that stocks are expected to earn over safe assets. Marks uses earnings yields and valuation ratios to argue that starting valuations shape forward returns. High prices mean lower expected returns, whatever the recent past suggests.
