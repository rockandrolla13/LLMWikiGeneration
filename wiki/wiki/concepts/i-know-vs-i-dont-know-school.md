# The 'I Know' and 'I Don't Know' Schools

## Definition

Marks's two camps of investors. The 'I know' school believes it can forecast the economy and markets and invests on that basis. The 'I don't know' school accepts the future is unknowable and focuses on value, risk and preparation. Marks places himself in the second camp.
