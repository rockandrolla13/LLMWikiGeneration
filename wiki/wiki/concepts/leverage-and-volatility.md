# Leverage and Volatility

## Definition

Leverage magnifies both gains and losses but does not improve the underlying investment. Marks's formula 'volatility plus leverage equals dynamite' summarises how borrowed money on volatile assets, especially with margin calls, turns price declines into ruin.
