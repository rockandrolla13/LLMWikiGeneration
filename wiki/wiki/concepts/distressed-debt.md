# Distressed Debt Investing

## Definition

Buying the debt of companies in or near default or bankruptcy, at prices below what the claims are likely to recover. It is a core Oaktree strategy. Marks treats it as a contrarian, cycle-dependent activity that does best when forced and fearful sellers dominate.
