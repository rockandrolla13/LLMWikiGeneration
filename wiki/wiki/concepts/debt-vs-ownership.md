# Debt vs Ownership

## Definition

Marks's view that there are only two basic asset classes: owning something (equity, real estate) or lending to someone (bonds, loans). Debt gives a contractual, mostly fixed outcome if the borrower pays, while ownership gives an uncertain residual. When credit yields approach historic equity returns, he argues the contractual return becomes the better bargain.
